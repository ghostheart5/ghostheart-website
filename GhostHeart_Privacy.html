Privacy and email

Your inbox stays yours.

This notice covers website visit analytics, the email address you choose to submit through the Join the GhostHeart Signal form, and the optional third-party film players on this website.

Website visit analytics

GhostHeart uses Metricool to measure visits and pages viewed on this website. Its tracking script connects to Metricool when a page loads. Metricool states that this tracker uses no cookies or unique visitor identifiers, does not store visitors' IP addresses, and provides anonymous, aggregate statistics. An IP address is processed during the connection to determine the visitor's country. These statistics help GhostHeart understand how the website is used. Learn more about Metricool's website tracker and its privacy practices.

Films and YouTube

GhostHeart film thumbnails load from this website. YouTube is not contacted until you press a film frame to load its player. When you do, the embedded privacy-enhanced YouTube player may receive connection and playback information under YouTube and Google's own privacy practices. You can browse the rest of the website without loading a YouTube player.

Optional reading bookmark

If you choose Save my place, this browser stores the page path and reading position on this device. It is not an account, is not sent to GhostHeart, and can be removed with Clear saved place. Email addresses are never stored by this bookmark.

What is collected

The signup form asks for your email address. GhostHeart does not ask for a password, payment information, or sensitive personal information through this form.

Why it is collected

Your email address is used to send GhostHeart updates such as new songs, films, stories, resources, and project news. Follow the confirmation instructions shown by Mailchimp after signup.

Who processes it

The form is processed by Mailchimp. When you submit it, Mailchimp receives the information and handles the confirmation and mailing-list record under its own privacy practices.

Your control

  • You can leave the form blank and continue using the rest of the website.
  • Submitting the website form alone is not a confirmation of delivery. Mailchimp manages your signup status and any confirmation step.
  • You can unsubscribe from any GhostHeart Signal email using the link included in that message.

No sale of signup information

GhostHeart does not sell the email address you provide through this signup form.
