Privacy and email

Your inbox stays yours.

This notice covers the email address you choose to submit through the Join the GhostHeart Signal form and the optional third-party film players on this website.

Films and YouTube

GhostHeart film thumbnails load from this website. YouTube is not contacted until you press a film frame to load its player. When you do, the embedded privacy-enhanced YouTube player may receive connection and playback information under YouTube and Google's own privacy practices. You can browse the rest of the website without loading a YouTube player.

What is collected

The signup form asks for your email address. GhostHeart does not ask for a password, payment information, or sensitive personal information through this form.

Why it is collected

Your email address is used to send GhostHeart updates such as new songs, films, stories, resources, and project news. You must confirm the subscription before messages are sent.

Who processes it

The form is processed by Mailchimp. When you submit it, Mailchimp receives the information and handles the confirmation and mailing-list record under its own privacy practices.

Your control

  • You can leave the form blank and continue using the rest of the website.
  • You can ignore the confirmation message and remain unsubscribed.
  • You can unsubscribe from any GhostHeart Signal email using the link included in that message.

No sale of signup information

GhostHeart does not sell the email address you provide through this signup form.
