From feeling unseen to helping someone move again.

The apps are separate products, but they share GhostHeart's purpose. They are designed to meet people where they are, reduce shame and chaos, and help them reclaim enough direction to keep going. ChronoSpark

When life is too loud to plan.

ChronoSpark is an adaptive life-planning and decision-support app. It helps turn an overloaded day into a manageable next move: clarify what matters, break goals into reachable steps, build routines, and recover when the plan changes.

Its difference is not more pressure or another perfect schedule. It is structure that adjusts to the person—so overwhelm can become direction without shame.

Shadowfire

When the feeling has no language yet.

Shadowfire is a private reflection and self-understanding experience. It creates room to write honestly, notice emotional patterns, name what is happening beneath the surface, and turn inner chaos into something a person can understand.

Its difference is privacy and depth. It is not a public feed and not a performance. It is a place to face the shadow without becoming trapped inside it.

The larger idea

A connected GhostHeart ecosystem—when it is ready.

The long-term vision is a group of focused experiences that help people move from being lost to being seen, supported, and capable of lifting someone else. ChronoSpark and Shadowfire stay distinct because planning and private reflection solve different problems. The connection is the mission, not forced account complexity.
