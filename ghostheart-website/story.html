Volume one · The becoming

The public chapters

The original story begins with a boy born carrying a visible light—and a world that taught him visibility could be dangerous.

Chapter 01 · Public

The Light

A boy was born with a heart that glowed. It was not a metaphor to the people around him. It was something they could see—and something they did not understand.

“Before he became a ghost, he was a light no one knew how to hold.”
Chapter 02 · Public

The Mask

Fear and judgment taught him that being seen could hurt. His mother protected him the way she knew how and taught him to cover the truth the world had made unsafe.

“Sometimes hiding is not surrender. Sometimes it is how a child survives.”
Chapter 03 · Public

The Fractures

Years of being misunderstood made different versions of him: the quiet one, the angry one, the drifter, the survivor. Each carried a piece the others could not.

“Broken did not mean finished.”
Chapter 04 · Public

The Spark

Then someone looked past the mask. She did not see fractures; she saw fire. She did not see a ghost; she saw a heart still fighting to be alive.

“She didn't rebuild him. She reminded him he was never destroyed.”
