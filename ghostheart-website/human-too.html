Why Human Too

More than a label or a mistake

Human Too is about addiction, judgment, pain, and remembering that a person is more than a label or a mistake.

People can carry grief, trauma, shame, hope, love, mistakes, and the need to belong. The message is compassion before judgment.

The Signal

See the person.
Not the label.

You can acknowledge harmful behavior without denying someone's humanity.

Compassion does not require pretending consequences do not exist.

A person's struggle is not their entire identity.

People deserve dignity while seeking appropriate help and support.
