You’re Not God

A question about belief, judgement and the difference between care and control.

Where does belief end and control begin?

You’re Not God explores that question through songs about judgement, shame and people who claim the right to speak for heaven. Its focus is what happens when faith is used to hurt or exclude someone.

That Ain’t Holy and Hey Preacher Man ask what happens when compassion becomes control and religion is used to excuse cruelty.

Belief can matter deeply to a person. That does not give someone else the right to decide whose love counts or whose life has value.

This collection is not an attack on faith. It is a confrontation with hypocrisy: purity demanded by people hiding their own sins, scripture used as a weapon, and personal hatred mistaken for God’s voice.

I Won’t Pretend to Speak for You tells it through one long love. One marriage began beneath a church-house light, surrounded by every blessing people could give. Two women began with trembling hands and borrowed flowers. Fifty years later, the song asks what a lifetime of love can tell us that appearances cannot.

That Ain’t Holy is a father-and-son story about poverty, dignity, and who gets judged. Mercy should not check your pockets at the door.

If your faith requires cruelty, that ain’t holy.

The album holds those ideas together: room for faith, and a boundary against cruelty carried out in its name.
