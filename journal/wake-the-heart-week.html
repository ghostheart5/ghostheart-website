Wake the Heart Week

November 9 to 15: one week inside Before the Heart Wakes, built around the songs and the people they are for.

From Monday, November 9 to Sunday, November 15, Before the Heart Wakes gets a week of its own: Wake the Heart Week.

Before the Heart Wakes is eight songs that follow one journey, from witnessing cruelty to finding the courage to stand beside someone else. For one week, every place GhostHeart shows up will carry a different piece of that same story. One heart. Never the same post twice.

Here is what is already set. On Saturday, November 14 at 7 PM Central, Passage 06 opens: the reading for This Is GhostHeart. On Sunday, November 15 at 10 AM Central, the week closes with the Sunday Live.

A Friday night listening event and the first letter to the email list are planned for the same week. Their details will be posted here and sent by email once they are confirmed.

You don’t have to take part in everything. Stay with one song. Carry one line. If someone comes to mind while you listen, that might be the person to share it with.

What survived can help someone rise.
