This is GhostHeart.

A heart that survived, and a question about what comes next.

This Is GhostHeart begins with a heart that survived and a question about what comes next. The song opens a larger story told through music, film, words and pictures.

What can a person carry forward from survival? And how might that become something another person can recognize themselves in?

The film calls it the name, the scars, and the reason a hidden heart chose to be seen.

In Enter My World, I put it this way: “Maybe my story could help somebody. Even with the ugly parts. Even with the mistakes I have to own.” And then: “If I help one person, then I did something.”

That is the heart of this song. It doesn’t ask anyone to share that past. It asks what a person might carry forward, and who might need to see it.

This Is GhostHeart is one of the eight songs on The Awakening. Its Passage, the longer reading for the song, opens Saturday, November 14 at 7 PM Central.
