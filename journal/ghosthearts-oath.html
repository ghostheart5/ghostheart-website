GhostHeart’s Oath

What might protecting the light in others look like in an ordinary moment?

GhostHeart’s Oath turns from the name to the promise behind it: protecting the light in others.

What might that promise look like in an ordinary moment? What survived can become a reason to help someone else rise.

The mission behind it fits in three words: Build. Serve. Change. Turn survival into work that leaves a door open for someone else.

GhostHeart turns survival into work that serves people who feel unseen. Build the platforms. Tell the truth. Leave a door open behind you.

Sometimes protecting the light might look like noticing who was left out. Sometimes it might mean giving someone the patience you once needed.

GhostHeart’s Oath is one of the eight songs on Before the Heart Wakes. Its Passage, the longer reading for the song, opens Saturday, November 21 at 7 PM Central.
