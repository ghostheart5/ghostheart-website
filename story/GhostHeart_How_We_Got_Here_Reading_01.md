# How We Got Here
## Reading 01 — GhostHeart

I wanted to make a place for people like me.

That was how I explained it. I wanted to help people who understood what I was talking about. People who needed somewhere to belong.

Then my wife challenged me.

She asked me to think about what I was building. If I wanted people to stop separating us, why was my answer another place that separated people?

She wanted me to see something bigger than the people whose lives looked like mine. There were good people I had not met. People who could help each other. People who needed love, even when their reasons were different from mine.

Helping people like me did not have to mean helping only people like me.

And she challenged me about something else: telling her I wanted to make a difference was not the same as doing it. I needed to take what I was saying and tell the world.

She got to me.

Not because I suddenly had every answer. I still do not. But the question had changed.

It was no longer only about making a place where I belonged. It was about what I could build that made room for somebody else, too.

I do not have to live your story to care about what happens to you. You do not have to live mine to understand why I am telling it.

That is part of how we got here.

Before I tell you everything GhostHeart carries, I need to tell you why I decided he should speak.
