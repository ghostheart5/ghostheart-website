Why Wake the Heart

Wake the Heart is about what happens when we let ourselves feel something—and let that feeling reach another person. Across fourteen weeks, I’m sharing songs about the things we carry, the words we struggle to say, and the moments that remind us we’re still here. Each week opens another part of that story. I hope you hear something of yourself in it and feel a little less alone.
