You don’t have to figure this out alone.

You can call, text, chat, or read first. There is no perfect way to begin. These are U.S.-based starting points.

988

Call or text 988 to talk with a crisis counselor, for you or someone you care about. Chat at chat.988lifeline.org ↗

911

Immediate danger. For an urgent threat to life or safety, a serious medical emergency, fire, or a crime in progress.

800-422-4453

Childhelp, 24/7, for questions or support about child abuse. Call or text.

GhostHeart is not a crisis, medical, legal, housing, or emergency service. These connect to outside organizations with their own services and privacy rules.

Your privacy · Your choice

What “confidential” means at 988

You can receive support without giving your name or location. Confidential does not mean anonymous: 988 may receive your phone number or chat IP address, calls may be monitored or recorded for quality assurance, and information can be shared in limited circumstances such as imminent risk or when required by law.

Need to leave this page quickly?

Internet, call, and text activity can sometimes be monitored and may not be completely erasable. Use a safer device if you can. Clear history only if doing so will not raise suspicion.
