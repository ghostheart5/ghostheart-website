Unlabeled

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

Unlabeled is an interactive story about identity and labels for Android, developed by GhostHeart Studios. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information the app stores

Unlabeled stores your place in the story, the choices you made and your reading preference (larger text) in private app storage on your device. This information is used only to let you continue where you left off and to show your journal of choices. The app does not collect or transmit this information to GhostHeart Studios or anyone else. It has no account, developer server, network connection, advertising, analytics or advertising identifier. Android cloud backup is disabled for Unlabeled.

What the app does not ask for

The story asks you to make choices for a fictional character. It never asks you to tell it anything about your own life, identity or relationships, and nothing you choose is sent anywhere.

Retention and deletion

Your progress stays on your device until you restart, clear the app's data in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Restarting replaces your saved path. Because Unlabeled does not send data to a developer server, GhostHeart Studios does not hold a copy to retrieve or delete.

Security

Story progress stays in Android private app storage. Device security, including screen lock and operating-system updates, is managed through your Android device.

Children's privacy

Unlabeled is a story for adults and is not designed for or directed to children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's information practices change, this policy will be updated and the effective date will change. The current version will be available at this address.

Privacy contact

For questions about this policy or Unlabeled's privacy practices, contact GhostHeart Studios at ghostheart131517@gmail.com.
