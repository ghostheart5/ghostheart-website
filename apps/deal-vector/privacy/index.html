Deal Vector

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

Deal Vector is an offline resale estimate calculator for Android, developed by GhostHeart Studios. This policy explains what information the app handles and what happens when you choose to copy or share an estimate.

Information the app stores

Deal Vector stores your current calculator draft and the calculations you choose to save in private app storage on your device. This information is used to restore your draft and display saved calculations. The app does not collect or transmit this information to GhostHeart Studios or operate an account or developer server. The app does not use advertising, analytics, or advertising identifiers. Android cloud backup and device transfer are disabled for Deal Vector.

Copying and sharing

If you tap Copy result, Android places the estimate on your device clipboard. If you tap Share result, Android opens its system sharing interface and you choose whether to send the estimate to another app or person. Deal Vector does not initiate these actions without your tap. Any recipient app or service handles information under its own privacy terms.

Retention and deletion

Your current draft remains in the app until replaced or the app is uninstalled. You can delete an individual saved calculation in the Saved tab. Reset replaces the current draft with example values. You can also clear Deal Vector's app data in Android settings or uninstall the app to remove its private local data. Because Deal Vector does not send data to a developer server, GhostHeart Studios does not hold a server copy to retrieve or delete.

Security

Calculator information stays in Android private app storage and is not sent to a developer-operated service. Device security, including screen lock and operating-system updates, is managed through your Android device.

Children's privacy

Deal Vector is a general-purpose resale calculator and is not designed to collect information from children. The app has no account, advertising, analytics, or network connection.

Changes to this policy

If the app's information practices change, this policy will be updated and the effective date will change. The current version will be available at this address.

Privacy contact
