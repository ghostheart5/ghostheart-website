Know what you are using

A small tool with clear limits.

Does it need an account or internet?

The current candidate works offline without an account, ads or analytics. Drafts and saved calculations stay in private app storage on your device. Opening the online privacy page uses your browser.

Can I edit and delete saved calculations?

Open Saved to load a named calculation or delete it. Loading changes the calculator draft. Keep any information you need before deleting a saved item.

What happens when I copy or share?

Copy result sends a summary to the Android clipboard. Share result opens Android’s share interface so you choose where to send it. The receiving app handles it under its own terms.

Can I download Deal Vector now?

A public launch has not been announced. Earlier FlipMath internal-test builds are not proof of current Deal Vector availability. This page does not offer an APK or a production download.
