Words That Hit

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

Words That Hit is a quote card and wallpaper maker for Android, developed by GhostHeart Studios. This policy explains what information the app handles and what happens when you choose to export or share a card.

Information the app stores

Words That Hit stores your current draft and the quotes you choose to save (the words, optional attribution, design, shape and text size) in private app storage on your device. This is used only to restore your draft and show your saved library. The app does not collect or transmit this information to GhostHeart Studios. It has no account, developer server, network connection, advertising, analytics or advertising identifier. Android cloud backup is disabled for Words That Hit.

Exporting and sharing

If you tap Export PNG, Android opens its file picker and you choose where to save the image. If you tap Share image or Share text, Android opens its sharing screen and you choose whether to send the card to another app or person. To share an image, the app writes that one image to a temporary folder in its private cache and grants the app you picked permission to read only that file. The app never exports or shares anything without your tap. Any app or service you send a card to handles it under its own privacy terms.

Retention and deletion

Your draft stays in the app until you replace it or uninstall the app. You can delete any saved quote from the Library tab. You can also clear the app's data in Android settings or uninstall the app to remove everything stored on your device. Because Words That Hit does not send data to a developer server, GhostHeart Studios does not hold a copy to retrieve or delete.

Security

Your quotes stay in Android private app storage. Device security, including screen lock and operating-system updates, is managed through your Android device.

Children's privacy

Words That Hit is a general-audience creative tool and is not designed to collect information from children. It has no account, advertising, analytics or network connection.

Changes to this policy

If the app's information practices change, this policy will be updated and the effective date will change. The current version will be available at this address.
