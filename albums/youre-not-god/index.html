Read · The album story

A note from GhostHeart.

YOU’RE NOT GOD is for everyone who has been judged, shamed, controlled, or cast aside by people claiming they speak for heaven.

This album is not an attack on faith. It is a confrontation with hypocrisy—the preacher demanding purity while hiding their own sins, the believer using scripture as a weapon, and anyone who mistakes personal hatred for God’s voice.

From That Ain’t Holy to Hey Preacher Man, these songs expose what happens when love is replaced by fear, compassion becomes control, and religion is used to excuse cruelty.

No human gets to decide whose life has value, whose love is real, or who deserves grace. You can stand behind a pulpit, a title, or a crowd—but you are still human.

If your faith requires cruelty, that ain’t holy.
If your God sounds exactly like your hate, maybe that’s your voice.

You can have your beliefs. You do not get to play God.

This is YOU’RE NOT GOD.

— GhostHeart
