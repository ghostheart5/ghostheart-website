Read · The album story

A note from GhostHeart.

LOVE IS LOVE is an album for every heart that has ever been told its love was wrong.

These songs ask one simple question: If love is honest, loyal, compassionate, and built to last, why should it be judged by who is holding hands?

Love that protects, forgives, remains faithful, and creates a home does not become less real—or less sacred—because someone else refuses to understand it.

This album isn’t asking for permission or approval. It is refusing to let judgment speak louder than truth. Love is not a label, a political argument, or a rule written by people who have never lived your story.

“They call it rebellion when you love who you want.”

Maybe the greatest rebellion is choosing love anyway.

Love doesn’t ask permission to exist. Love is love.

— GhostHeart
