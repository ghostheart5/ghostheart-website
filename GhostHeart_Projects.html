Planning and decision support

Axiomara

Understand the day. Decide the next move.

For people carrying too much at once. Axiomara is a planning and decision-support system designed to help people understand the day, decide the next move, and maintain momentum—while keeping every decision in the user's hands.

  • Understand
  • Decide
  • Advance
Read the mission

Connect tasks, goals, Daily Rhythms, and notes with useful planning and decision support. Help people understand the day, choose their next move, and maintain momentum. Guidance supports the user; it does not replace their judgment.

Current stage: being actively built—shown here as a mission, not a finished release.

Private reflection system

Shadowfire

When the feeling has no language yet.

For people who need a private place to understand what they feel. Shadowfire is a journal and reflection experience for turning inner chaos into language.

  • Reflect
  • Name
  • Understand
Read the mission

Create room to write honestly, notice emotional patterns, and name what is happening beneath the surface—without performing it for a public feed.

Current stage: in development—privacy and honest reflection remain central to the direction.
